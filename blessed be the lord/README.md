# Cape Meridian Arms

A responsive, South Africa-oriented sporting-goods storefront prototype built with plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

## Preview

Open `index.html` directly in a browser, or use the VS Code Live Server extension. No package installation or build step is required.

## Features

- Responsive home, catalogue, product detail, about, contact/FAQ, account, accessory bag, and order-enquiry pages.
- Catalogue search, category filters, sorting, load-more, product galleries, and browser-local wishlists.
- Browser-local accessory bag with quantity controls and subtotal; regulated listings are never added to the bag.
- Firearm listings are enquiry-only. This site has no firearm purchase, reservation, checkout, or payment flow.
- Age declaration for users 21 and older, plus clear notice that it is not age, identity, licence, or eligibility verification.
- Contact, newsletter, account, and enquiry forms are demonstration-only and do not transmit or persist form submissions.

## Before launch

This is a front-end prototype, not a production commerce or dealer-verification system. Replace the sample business name, contact details, placeholder product listings/images, and company copy with verified retailer information and licensed product data.

Do not treat the age declaration as an access-control or compliance mechanism. A licensed South African dealer must independently verify age, identity, eligibility, required competency and licences, inventory, and every applicable legal requirement before any regulated transaction proceeds. The website does not provide legal advice.

Secure account authentication, profile and enquiry history, staff roles, admin inventory/enquiry workflows, protected image uploads, audit logs, server-side validation, order processing, and payment processing require a secure backend. The account forms and staff portal intentionally do not simulate security. Do not enter real credentials or personal documents on this prototype.

Accessory bag data is stored in the browser's local storage and is not an order. The product imagery is illustrative placeholder photography; verify licensing and replace it with accurate product photography before launch. Fonts and images load from external services.
